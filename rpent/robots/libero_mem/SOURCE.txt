LIBERO-Mem evaluation overlay.

Upstream eval env: https://github.com/libero-mem/libero-mem
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11478
Site: https://libero-mem.github.io/

This overlay does NOT vendor their full `libero` package and must not be
pip-installed into /root/autodl-tmp/envs/rpent (that would replace
liberopro). Only the sparse clone is used:

  libero/libero/bddl_files/libero_mem/*.bddl
  scripts/__init_data/*.pruned_init
  sequential checker logic (ported into robots/libero_mem/sequential.py)

Kitchen meshes stay on the existing LIBERO-PRO assets. Do not download
https://github.com/libero-mem/naive-e-slotssm or the 211GB HF dataset.
